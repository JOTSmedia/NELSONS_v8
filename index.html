The family corner

The yellow sign you look for on Court Street.

Martinez Grocery is a Carroll Gardens bodega that still runs like one: a counter, a beer case, flowers out front, and someone who will hold a plant until you can come back for it.

Come by for a sandwich. Call if you want it brought to you — free delivery is painted on the sign, not buried in an app.

Guardians

Mars has passed. Sacha is missing.

Mars · Passed

Mars has passed.

Mars kept the sidewalk. In the summer he sat by the buckets and watched Court Street. In the winter he slept inside, on his bed next to the radiator. He is missed.

“He sat with the mums.”

Sacha · Missing

Sacha is missing.

Black coat, white chest, yellow eyes, pink collar. Sacha was a fixture in the flowers until he went missing. If you see him, call the shop.

“If you see the pink collar, call the shop.”
